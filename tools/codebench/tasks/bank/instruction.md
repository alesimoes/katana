Account in bank.py has two bugs. (1) Withdrawing exactly the balance raises InsufficientFunds; it must succeed (only withdrawing MORE than the balance raises). (2) balance is a float, so depositing 0.1 and 0.2 gives 0.30000000000000004; the balance must be exact: store it as decimal.Decimal built from str(amount), so the result equals Decimal('0.3'). Deposits and withdrawals of zero or negative amounts raise ValueError. Fix it.
