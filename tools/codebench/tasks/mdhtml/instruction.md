mdhtml.py has a stub render(text). Implement a converter from a Markdown subset to HTML that returns a string. Blocks: (1) a line starting with one to six # followed by a space is a heading: <h1>..</h1> to <h6>..</h6>; (2) consecutive lines starting with "- " form <ul><li>a</li><li>b</li></ul>; consecutive lines starting with a number, a dot and a space ("1. ", "2. ") form <ol><li>..</li></ol>; (3) a fenced block between two lines that start with ``` becomes <pre><code>...</code></pre>: its lines are kept verbatim and joined with a newline, blank lines inside are kept, the text is HTML-escaped and gets no inline formatting; (4) any other consecutive lines form a paragraph <p>..</p>, the lines stripped and joined by one space. A paragraph ends at a blank line or at a line that starts another kind of block. Inline rules, applied in headings, list items and paragraphs: first escape & < > as &amp; &lt; &gt;, then `code` becomes <code>code</code> (nothing inside code is formatted further), **bold** becomes <strong>bold</strong>, *italic* becomes <em>italic</em> and [text](url) becomes <a href="url">text</a>. The result is the HTML of the blocks joined by a single newline, with no trailing newline. Empty or whitespace-only input returns an empty string.
