This project (package agenda/, tests in tests/) is a small personal calendar. Add recurring events without breaking the current behaviour or the existing tests. (1) Event gets two optional fields: `repeat` (None, "daily", "weekly" or "monthly") and `until` (a YYYY-MM-DD string, inclusive, or None for no end); both are saved in the JSON file, and old files without them mean a plain event that happens once. (2) New module agenda/recurrence.py with `occurrences(event, start, end)`: the list of dates (YYYY-MM-DD strings, ascending) on which the event happens inside [start, end], both inclusive. A plain event happens once, on its own date. A recurring event begins on its own date (never earlier) and repeats every day, every 7 days, or on the same day of the month, and stops after `until`. For monthly, a day that does not exist in a month (the 31st in April, the 29th to 31st in February) falls on the last day of that month, but every month is computed from the original day: Jan 31 gives Feb 28, Mar 31, Apr 30. (3) Calendar.add(title, day, time, minutes, repeat=None, until=None) validates the new arguments and raises AgendaError when repeat is not one of the three values, when until is given without repeat, when until is not a valid date or is before the event's date. on_day, between and conflicts must take recurring events into account: a weekly event shows up on each of its dates, and two events conflict on a day when their occurrences on that day overlap. (4) In the CLI, `add` accepts `--repeat daily|weekly|monthly` and `--until DATE`, and in the output of `day` and `range` the line of a recurring event ends with the repeat word in brackets, like `09:30-09:45 Standup (id 2) [weekly]`; everything else in the output stays as it is.
